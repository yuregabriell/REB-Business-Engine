# SaaS — sector prompt pack

Investigate user workflow, segment, switching cost, willingness to pay, activation, retention, support load, infrastructure costs, security obligations, unit economics, platform dependencies, integration risks and price testing.

Measure: activation, churn, net retention, gross margin, cost of serving a customer, acquisition cost, payback period and support burden. Do not infer product-market fit from signup counts or waiting lists alone. Engage REA/RES only through installed approved capability contracts.
