# Local services — sector prompt pack

Ask about location, accessibility, radius of service, scheduling, transport, labor, materials, insurance, supplier obligations, customer expectations, reviews, cancellations and repeat purchases.

Measure: price per service, variable cost per service, utilization per staff hour, travel cost, customer acquisition cost, repeat rate, complaints, conversion from quote, cancellations and actual cash collection.

Validate with small paid orders before purchasing expensive assets; distinguish customer promises from successful delivery. Source regulatory requirements from official country and municipality sources; do not imply a single set of rules applies across jurisdictions.
