# REB Knowledge Scientist — initial method library

These are **method candidates**, not universal success formulas. No book text is copied. Each card has source, action, evidence grade, applicability and limitation.

## Method cards

### Evidence-based hypothesis testing
- Source: David J. Bland / Alexander Osterwalder, *Testing Business Ideas*, Strategyzer, https://www.strategyzer.com/library/testing-business-ideas-book
- Application: rank the most consequential unproven assumptions, run affordable experiments, observe behavior and document outcomes.
- Evidence type: practitioner framework; effectiveness is context-dependent.
- Do not infer paid demand solely from signups.

### Customer conversations that avoid leading questions
- Source: Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test*, https://www.momtestbook.com/
- Application: ask about previous decisions, current substitutes and concrete budgets; avoid questions inviting politeness.
- Evidence type: practitioner book.
- Limitation: interviews alone do not prove conversion or willingness to pay.

### Small-market hands-on delivery
- Source: Paul Graham, *Do Things that Don't Scale*, https://paulgraham.com/ds.html
- Application: perform manual initial service to learn; automate only after discovering a repeated valuable workflow.
- Evidence type: founder narrative and essays, not causal proof.
- Limitation: some regulated/complex services are not suitable for manual shortcuts.

### Personal-initiative training
- Source: Campos et al., *Teaching personal initiative beats traditional training in boosting small business in West Africa*, Science (2017), https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aan5329
- Application: encourage concrete self-starting actions and obstacle analysis.
- Evidence type: randomized controlled trial in Togo.
- Limitation: reported profit improvements in that context should NOT be promised for others.

### Scientific startup decision-making
- Source: Camuffo et al., *A Scientific Approach to Entrepreneurial Decision Making: Evidence from a Randomized Control Trial*, Management Science (2020), https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3249
- Application: state a hypothesis, run tests, define disconfirmation conditions, pivot or persist based on observed evidence.
- Evidence type: randomized experiment in a specific startup setting.
- Limitation: not a universal guarantee, other variables impact outcomes.

### Lean iterative learning
- Source: Eric Ries, *The Lean Startup*, https://theleanstartup.com/
- Application: small measured build-measure-learn cycles.
- Evidence type: practitioner account.
- Limitation: a minimum prototype is not necessarily the right validation method for every industry.

### Customer acquisition channel experimentation
- Source: Gabriel Weinberg / Justin Mares, *Traction*, https://tractionbook.com/
- Application: test a limited portfolio of acquisition channels with a budget and success criteria.
- Evidence type: practitioner synthesis.
- Limitation: market, channel costs, platform rules and reach change.

## Knowledge scientist quality rubric

1. Randomized trials / transparent systematic analysis with sample and caveats.
2. Multiple independent documented case studies with underlying measurements.
3. Single documented founder account with real outcomes, treated as correlational.
4. Practitioner methodology with explicit assumptions.
5. Unsupported assertion: hypothesis only, not adopted as proven.

Record publication time, sample, market, intervention, measurement window, biases, alternate explanations and quality of source. Keep contrary evidence. Re-evaluate old cases against present conditions before recommending expensive decisions.
